# Add physics areas to tilemaps

To make bodies collide with the tiles of a tilemap or detect them as triggers, add a [2D Physics Core](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.2d.physics@latest) physics area to the tilemap with the Physics Area Tilemap component. The shapes of the physics area follow the tilemap as you paint, erase, or change its tiles, in the Unity Editor and at runtime.
